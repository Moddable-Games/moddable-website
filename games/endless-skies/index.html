Endless Skies

4X space exploration with worker placement and fleet management. Command asymmetric factions across a hex-tile galaxy, placing crew in ship rooms and diplomats in space ports. Based on the open-source video game Endless Sky.

How it plays

CORE MECHANICS

Worker placement meets 4X exploration

Four interlocking systems drive every round of Endless Skies.

FACTIONS

Eight factions. Five tech trees. Asymmetric starts

Each faction has unique starting credits, technology access, and strategic pressure. Some scale with player count, others have fixed wealth regardless of game size.
