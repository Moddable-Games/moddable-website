Nukes

Cold-war territory control on a hex map. Return hostages to move — but every return reinforces your enemy. Build bases, convert isotopes, and threaten nuclear annihilation. Designed to be modded from day one.

How it plays

NUCLEAR STRIKES

Launch from a Base.
Destroy everything

Convert units into isotopes. Launch from any Base. Jump over occupied hexes like Artillery. The target hex becomes permanent Biohazard — all units in the target and all six surrounding hexes are destroyed immediately. Including your own.

CORE MECHANICS

Simple components. Emergent complexity

44 hex tiles and 75 tokens — no miniatures, no dice, no hidden information.
