Planet Mongo

Hex-based territory control set in the Flash Gordon universe. 127 hex tiles, 8 asymmetric factions, hidden simultaneous orders, and a central commodity market. Designed to combine the best of Twilight Imperium and Game of Thrones: The Board Game, with deeper bluffing and trade.

How it plays

IN DEVELOPMENT

Open design. Rules not yet published

Planet Mongo is being designed in the open. Factions, unit stats, and the initiative matrix are defined. Innovation cards, event decks, and victory conditions are still being finalised through playtesting. The hex map generator is live now.

CORE MECHANICS

Simultaneous orders. Hidden stacks. Dynamic economy

Four interlocking systems drive every round of Planet Mongo.

FACTIONS

Eight rulers. Eight regions. Eight ways to win

Each faction has a unique objective, starting position, VP formula, and stat distribution across 8 axes: Wealth, Population, Military, Science, Culture, Morale, Defense, and Biomes.
