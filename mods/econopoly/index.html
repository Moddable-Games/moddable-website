Econopoly

Transforms Monopoly into a euro-game with resource collection, blind auctions, and victory point scoring. Choose to move, trade, or build each turn. No more player elimination marathons.

How it plays

THE DECKS

Four suits. Two decks. Infinite sessions

Events change the rules mid-game. Resources combine into scoring sets. Every suit has a distinct strategic flavour.

What you'll need

MOD HOOKS

Rewrite the economy. Redesign the decks

Every card, scoring rule, and station mechanic is defined in the rulebook.
