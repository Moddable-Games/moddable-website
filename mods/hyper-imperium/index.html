Hyper Imperium

Five modular rule changes for Twilight Imperium 4e + Prophecy of Kings. 3D exploration via hidden systems, free trading with promissory notes, shared secret objectives, custom factions, and blind agenda voting.

How it plays

FACTIONS & MODULES

Two new factions. One faction builder

Play the pre-built Remnant or Wanderers, build a custom faction from 100 points, or mix any combination of the five Hyper modules.

GENERATE

Explore the galaxy

Generate Hyper Imperium maps with the extra outer ring. Powered by Moddable Hexmaps.

MOD HOOKS

Design your own faction. Rewrite the agendas

Every deck, faction ability, and point-buy option is defined in the rulebook.
