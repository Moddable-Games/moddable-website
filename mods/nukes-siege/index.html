Nukes: Siege

Total conversion of the hostage mechanic. Start with your own tokens in reserve and recruit by returning them to occupied regions. Enemy hostages only arrive once combat starts.

How it plays

WHAT CHANGES

Earn your hostages. Don’t start with them

Standard Nukes hands you leverage from turn one. Siege makes you earn it. The hostage economy still runs — you just have to build the supply yourself before you can spend it.
