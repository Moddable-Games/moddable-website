Talisman Worlds

Replaces the Talisman game boards with 61 hexagonal tiles arranged in concentric rings. Every session lays out differently. Built for Talisman 4th Edition (revised).

How it plays

THE BOARD

Four rings. One hidden ending

Each ring has its own movement rules, tile types, and access restrictions. Navigate inward to reach the ending.

GENERATE

A new board every session

Powered by Moddable Hexmaps. Click hexes to reassign terrain rings.

MOD HOOKS

Remix the world. Rewrite the journey

Every tile, ending, and movement rule is defined in the rulebook and hex generator.
